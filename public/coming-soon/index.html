Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Can I sign up now if you haven't launched yet?

Yes. You can register today, complete your profile and configure your availability — all before we go live publicly to patients. When we launch, your profile is ready to take bookings on day one.

When is MyDoctors360 opening to patients?

Coming soon. Founding doctors can sign up now. Patients can join the waitlist with a name and email.

What does it cost?

Founding (£99/mo, first 100): Solo Professional features — bookings, video, payments, analytics, CRM and waitlist.

Starter (£199/mo): solo — online bookings & Stripe payouts, video, email reminders.

Professional (£299/mo, 1 doctor): solo growth — everything in Starter plus advanced analytics, patient CRM and waitlist auto-notify.

Clinic: for multi-doctor practices. Get in touch for pricing.

Annual billing is 10 months for 12 (2 months free). Upgrade or change plan anytime from your dashboard. Full matrix on pricing.

Prices exclude VAT. VAT will only be added if we become VAT-registered.

Solo practice or multi-doctor clinic?

Solo doctors use the founding plan, Starter or Professional — each is a single doctor seat. Multi-doctor practices use Clinic (3 seats included, up to 15 on one bill). Prefer independent accounts? Use Referrals so colleagues sign up on their own plan — that does not use your seats. Not sure which fits? See pricing or the package helper on contact.

Who can register?

Verified, regulated doctors and clinicians practising privately in the United Kingdom. Clinics and group practices in the United Kingdom are welcome.

How is this different from existing platforms?

We're building from the ground up with input from working doctors. Modern tooling, transparent pricing, multilingual reach and genuinely useful automation — not a directory bolted onto a billing system.
